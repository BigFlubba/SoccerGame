# The Road After the Park — Design

Amends `2026-10-22-harrison-soccer-design.md` (the main spec) and the
*Build order change* in `2026-10-27-the-park-design.md` (Plan 7b). Where
they disagree, this document wins. Anything it doesn't touch still
stands.

## Intent

Plan 7b is live (`main` at `f4953c8`). Harrison played the Park through
to Speedy Sam on Easy and says the game "feels really fun now". His
requests:

- new moves: the **rainbow flick** and **stepovers**;
- longer venues: the Park was too short ("he beat it pretty fast"), and
  he wants more matches before each boss;
- **customizing his look**;
- **more upgrades**, such as stamina;
- **multiple save games**, so he can start over.

The parent agreed the changes below on 2026-10-27 ("This all sounds
perfect!"). This document reshapes the rest of the career and the order
of the next plans. Each item still gets its own design session before
it's built, as the Park did.

## What was decided

By the parent:

- **The Backyard and the Park don't change.** Everything below is for
  the venues still to come.
- **Every remaining venue has 8 matches and a boss**, double the main
  spec's four.
- **Opponents are a mix.** Harrison prefers more different opponents,
  so most are new faces. To keep the number of new opponents
  manageable, some matches bring back an earlier opponent who is
  tougher now. The exact mix is settled in each venue's own design.
- **A new venue, the State Championship,** goes between the School
  Field and the City Stadium. Its look, format and boss were left to the
  designer, and the proposal below was accepted as is.
- **The next plans, in order:** skill moves; save slots with a Stamina
  upgrade; the Locker Room's first round; Quick Match; then the venues.

## The career, six venues

| # | Venue | Format | Matches | Boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Backyard (unchanged) | 1v1, open goals | 10 rungs | Biscuit the Dog |
| 2 | The Park (unchanged) | 1v1, open goals | 4 | Speedy Sam |
| 3 | School Field | 1v1 + keepers | 8 | Goalie Gus |
| 4 | **State Championship** (new) | 1v1 + keepers | 8 | Showtime Shay |
| 5 | City Stadium | 2v2 + keepers | 8 | The Twins |
| 6 | World Cup Final | 2v2 + keepers | 8 | The Golden Boot champion and partner |

- Matches unlock in order by winning, and the boss unlocks once match 8
  is beaten, as in the Park. Stars stay 1 / 2 / 3 per match.
- **Stars:** each new venue has 27 (8 matches and a boss, three stars
  each). The career total goes from the main spec's 93 to 156 (Backyard
  33, Park 15, four venues of 27). *Designer's proposal:* the coin-paying
  star milestones (10 / 25 / 50 / 75) gain a step or two (for example 100
  and 125) so they keep coming; amounts and steps are the builder's.
- **Saves:** nothing to do now. Each venue's matches are new catalog
  entries when the venue is built, appended as the save code's rules
  require.
- **Returning opponents** keep their look and personality and come back
  with better stats and a harder profile. They can come from earlier
  venues; a Park kid can turn up at the School Field. Each venue's design
  says which.

## The State Championship

*Designer's proposal, accepted by the parent.*

- **The place:** a real regional stadium, the step up from the school. A
  grandstand along one side with a crowd waving flags, a scoreboard, plain
  banners with no sponsors, and the State Cup trophy on a stand by the
  touchline. Late afternoon, long shadows, the floodlights just coming on:
  bigger than the School Field, not yet the City Stadium at night.
- **How it plays:** one-on-one with goalkeepers, as at the School Field,
  against tougher players and sharper keepers. The City Stadium stays
  where two-on-two starts.
- **A tournament:** the eight matches are named as rounds: GROUP 1 to 4,
  ROUND OF 32, ROUND OF 16, QUARTERFINAL, SEMIFINAL. The boss match is
  THE FINAL. *Left to its design:* whether the knockout rounds treat a
  draw differently from other venues.
- **The boss, Showtime Shay:** captain of the defending champions, who
  beats defenders with stepovers and rainbow flicks. The lesson: don't
  bite on the trick, stay goal-side and wait for the ball to move. With
  skill moves built first, Harrison will have learned those tricks
  himself. The name stands unless the parent changes it at the sheet.

## The next plans

### 1. Skill moves

- The **stepover** and the **rainbow flick**, as the main spec describes
  them: RB does a skill move, a stepover by default and a rainbow flick
  with the stick up (flicking the ball over an opponent's head), each
  unlocked by level. They are pulled forward from the main spec's build
  step 9.
- The bicycle kick and scorpion kick stay in step 9, with the other
  power moves and forms.
- Every opponent already in the game plays fair against them. Max,
  Biscuit, the crew and Sam play as before otherwise, and their tuning
  tests pass unchanged. The design session decides how each opponent
  reacts to a trick.

### 2. Save slots and a Stamina upgrade

- **Save slots:** a small number of slots (designer's guess: three),
  picked when the game starts, so Harrison can start a new career without
  losing his current one. Today's save loads into the first slot,
  losing nothing.
- **Stamina:** a fifth stat in the Shop alongside Speed, Power, Jump and
  Control. It changes how long his sprint lasts and how fast it comes
  back.
- **Both change the save, so they ship together in one save version.**
  The rules that bind every save still hold: fields are only ever
  appended, nothing earned or bought is taken back, storage that throws
  never stops the game, and old save codes still apply.
- The design session decides how Stamina enters the opponents' tuning
  measures; the scripted Harrisons' stats are pinned today.

### 3. The Locker Room, first round

- Customizing his look with **kits** (number 12 always), **boot colours**,
  and **accessories**: headband, wristbands, captain's armband,
  clear-framed glasses. Bought with coins, equipped in a Locker Room off
  the Hub. Pulled forward from the main spec's build step 9.
- **His hair and face don't change:** Pixel Harrison is the parent's
  approved likeness.
- Balls, aura colours, celebrations and the Trophy Room stay in step 9.

### 4. Quick Match

As the Park spec deferred it: built after the Park, before the next
venue. Its design session decides the rest.

### 5. Onward

The School Field, the State Championship, the City Stadium and the World
Cup Final, each in its own design session, then the rest of the main
spec's build order.

## Non-goals

- Changing the Backyard or the Park in any way, including their match
  counts.
- Changing Harrison's hair or face.
- Designing any later venue's opponents, or a returning opponent's
  numbers, here. Each venue's design session does that.

## Constraints

Unchanged from the earlier specs:

- the save rules above, and the no-kit parity rule;
- on-screen text uses only uppercase letters, digits and
  `' - : ! ? . , / + < >`;
- no image, audio or font files, and no surname anywhere;
- new art and any opponent's look go to the parent for approval;
- ask before every merge into `main` and every push, since Pages deploys
  on push.
