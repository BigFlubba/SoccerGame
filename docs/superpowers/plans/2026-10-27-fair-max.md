# A Fairer Max Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make Easy and Normal winnable against Max (a steal button, slides that never clear toward Harrison's goal, room at his kickoff, a drag-back turn, a retune) while Hard plays as tough as today, all measured headlessly in CI.

**Architecture:** Two new focused modules: `src/game/steal.ts` (Max's-ball test, the steal's reach, odds and outcome) and `src/game/dragBack.ts` (start and step the turn). `stepWorld` calls them for Harrison only. Per-difficulty values (clean-steal odds, wrong-footed beat, kickoff spot) live on Max's `AiProfile` and reach the world through a new `World.maxProfile`. Max's brain gains the kickoff hold and the wrong-footed beat. A scripted-Harrison harness `src/game/maxScripts.ts` and `src/game/maxTuning.test.ts` mirror `dogScripts.ts` / `dogTuning.test.ts`.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript, Vite, Vitest; headless simulation in `src/game`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-fair-max-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- On-screen text uses only uppercase letters, digits and `' - : ! ? . , / + < >`. No parentheses.
- No image, audio or font files; no new sprite frames (reuse existing frames). A new frame would need the parent's approval first.
- No save changes: the profile stays v3.
- The Energy Wave's rules and Max's back-off against a powered-up Harrison are unchanged.
- Biscuit is unchanged: `DOG_DIFFICULTY`, his kickoff spots and his knock-out pop stay; `src/game/dogTuning.test.ts` stays green without editing Biscuit's profiles or its bands (fix the scripted Harrisons in `dogScripts.ts` if needed).
- Max gets none of the new tools: no steal, no drag-back, no side-clear.
- Work on branch `fair-max`. Ask the parent before merging into `main` and before pushing (Pages deploys from `main`).
- The full suite and `npm run build` stay green at every commit.

## Review Focus

1. **A steal press when it can't apply** (charging, in the air, sliding, fallen, powered up, outside play): nothing happens, no stumble, no cooldown. Pinned in Task 2.
2. **The drag-back by a board, a post or a net:** the ball is never placed inside a goal or through a fence (`canPlace`); if it can't be placed the turn ends and the ball stays where it was. Pinned in Task 5.
3. **Changing difficulty in Options mid-match:** the next kickoff uses the new difficulty's spot and odds (`World.maxProfile` updated with the brain's profile). Pinned in Task 4.
4. **A clean steal while the ball is squeezed between both bodies:** no exception, the ball ends at Harrison's feet, and the 50/50 squirt doesn't immediately undo it that tick. Pinned in Task 2.
5. **Harrison never plays the ball at his kickoff:** Max comes after the hold timeout, and the ball never sits still for long (the tuning test's stuck check covers whole matches). Pinned in Task 4.
